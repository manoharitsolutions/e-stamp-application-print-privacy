Privacy Policy

Product: Stamp Application Print

Developer: Manohar IT Solutions

Effective date: 10 October 2026

1. Purpose

Stamp Application Print is a Chrome extension designed to help users retrieve relevant information from supported e-Stamp certificate pages, fill application forms, and prepare print-ready documents.

2. Information Processed

The extension may process relevant certificate details, form values entered by users, saved presets, and preferences to provide its autofill and printing features.

If account and subscription features are enabled, account details, authentication information, approval status, and subscription validity may also be processed.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the extension's stated features, manage accounts and subscriptions where applicable, and maintain service security.

4. Storage and Sharing

Form values and preferences may be stored locally in the browser. Account and subscription information may be processed by the service's server if those features are implemented.

Personal information is not sold. Data is shared only when necessary to operate the service, comply with law, or meet applicable privacy requirements.

5. Security and Retention

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information. Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes and applicable legal obligations.

6. User Choices

Users should provide only information necessary for the extension's functionality. Requests concerning personal data may be sent to the developer using the contact details on the Chrome Web Store listing.

7. Contact

Developer: Manohar IT Solutions

Please use the developer contact details listed on the Chrome Web Store page for privacy-related questions.
